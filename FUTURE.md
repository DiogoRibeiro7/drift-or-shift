# Future Work Appendix

These extensions outline what to tackle after the current milestones to keep drift-or-shift relevant to new datasets, drift regimes, and evaluation methodologies.

1. **Broader datasets & domain gaps.** Exp8 now provides a synthetic multi-modal benchmark, Exp9 adds Covertype, and Exp10 covers credit card fraud; next iterate on other high-variance financial or medical benchmarks so decision boundaries can be highly non-linear or class covariances shift more drastically. *Learning goal: assess whether logit offsets remain accurate when decision boundaries become non-linear or when feature covariances differ per class.*
2. **Extended drift types.** Exp7 now sweeps covariance scaling, feature shifts, and label noise while reporting density-ratio drift scores; next, extend the monitors (e.g., training-time feature statistics or multivariate KS tests) to see if more subtle drifts can be flagged earlier. *Learning goal: map which drift types break the single-scalar offset and quantify when retraining or domain adaptation becomes mandatory.*
3. **Calibration & thresholds.** Use the new calibration experiment (Exp6) to compare temperature scaling and isotonic regression before adding offsets, and iterate on the learning goal of whether calibrated probabilities plus offsets shrink oracle risk gaps when base logistic scores are miscalibrated. *Learning goal: determine whether calibration plus offsets recovers the test posteriors even when the base logistic scores are miscalibrated.*
4. **Benchmark automation & reporting.** Build a lightweight dashboard (e.g., Jupyter notebook or CLI report) that compiles the latest `results/*/tables` into summary figures and picks best-performing strategies automatically. *Learning goal: help future contributors quickly verify whether new experiments confirm the paper without rerunning everything.*
5. **Drift monitoring & alerts.** Surface the new `feature_max_mean_diff`, `feature_max_ks`, and mean/std deltas in dashboards or CI so huge shifts in covariances/means trigger reviewers before experiments run. *Learning goal: teach the pipeline to warn when instrumental features drift faster than the offset hypothesis allows, and to recommend when to retrain or re-calibrate before overnight runs.*
6. **Automation for dashboard refresh & gating.** Hook `scripts/aggregate_results.py` (and its best-risk figure) plus `scripts/drift_alerts.py` into a lightweight watcher or CI step so the reviewers' dashboard, alerts CSV, and digest always reflect the newest plots/tables as soon as any experiment finishes. *Learning goal: keep the narrative for reviewers in sync with the freshest experiments without manual bookkeeping.*
7. **Guided follow-up for calibration/drift insights.** Capture the newest calibration-vs-offset ratios, drift-alert summaries, and best-risk recommendations inside a digest (markdown + figure + CSV) that explicitly calls out when recalibration or retraining becomes necessary, letting the pipeline surface countermeasures for the experiments that most violate the offset assumptions. *Learning goal: make each new experiment suggest the next defensive action (retrain, recalibrate, or gather more data) in addition to reporting metrics.*
